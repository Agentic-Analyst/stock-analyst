Change the World 2023

The eighth-biggest company in the world by revenue is rapidly going back to the basics of sustainability—reduce, reuse, and recycle. In recent years, Apple has made it a priority to build out its environmental efforts, from reducing the plastic in its packaging to developing robots that better recover materials from discarded electronics. It plans for every product it makes to be carbon-neutral by 2030. Last year, 20% of materials in Apple products were from recycled or renewable sources, and the company intends to eventually reach 100%. The company recently announced its first-ever carbon-neutral Apple Watch and discontinued the use of leather in its product lines. Since 2015, the iPhone maker has cut emissions by more than 45% while its revenue increased by 68%, and its corporate operations are fully carbon-neutral.
