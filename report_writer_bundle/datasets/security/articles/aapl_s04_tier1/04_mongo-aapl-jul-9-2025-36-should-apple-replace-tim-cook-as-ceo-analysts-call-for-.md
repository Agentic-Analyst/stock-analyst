First, its chief financial officer left. Now, its chief operating officer will depart as well. But does Apple Inc. need an even more dramatic change in leadership?

That’s the view of LightShed partners analysts Walter Piecyk and Joe Galone, who wrote Wednesday that Apple AAPL seems due for a new chief executive. Their note comes a day after the company announced that Jeff Williams will soon retire as chief operating officer, to be replaced by an Apple veteran.
