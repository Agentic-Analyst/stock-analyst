Before everyone jumps the gun — it’s all publicly available and in the works since March. As much as 6 million shares are about to get dumped by the main man.

💰 $800 Million Cash-Out

Nvidia stock NVDA was showing a modest pre-market loss Friday after news broke out that the company’s main man was planning to cash out. On schedule, planned, and transparent.

Nvidia’s chief executive Jensen Huang on Wednesday filed to sell up to 6 million shares through a Rule 10b5-1 plan adopted back in March — a prearranged schedule designed to avoid accusations of insider trading.

The plan hasn’t kicked in yet, but it runs through the end of 2025, offering plenty of runway for timed liquidations.

🤫 Been There, Done That

Huang used the same strategy last year, when he sold 6 million shares between June and September. That netted him $713 million , at an average share price of $118.83 — pocket change by today’s Nvidia standards.

netted him $713 million With Nvidia now hovering near $140 a pop, this year’s sale could rake in just over $800 million. Shares of the chip titan just recently surfaced above the flatline for the year, flashing a tiny 0.6% year-to-date gain.

None of this suggests Huang, who’s among the longest-standing tech CEOs, is losing faith in his own company. Executives often schedule sales to diversify portfolios and lock in long-term gains — especially after a stock like Nvidia posts blowout earnings figures .
